## 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

As a **QA Management Lead and Automation Engineer**, I specialize in establishing robust quality engineering practices and building scalable automation frameworks. My focus lies in aligning software quality with business objectives, optimizing team workflows, and driving the "Shift-Left" testing culture across complex System Integration and Maintenance projects.

### 🎯 Core Competencies
- **Quality Engineering & Strategy:** Defining QA metrics, non-functional compliance, and designing unified workflows for Risk & Issue management via Jira.
- **Test Automation Architecture:** Designing scalable Page Object Model (POM) and Data-Driven frameworks utilizing Playwright, Cypress, and Appium.
- **Project Health & Leadership:** Orchestrating test cycles, distributing team tasks, and building KPI/Quality Health Dashboards.
- **Innovation & AI:** Leveraging automation scripting (Python/OpenCV) and AI tools (Claude Code CLI) to accelerate test case generation and framework refactoring.
